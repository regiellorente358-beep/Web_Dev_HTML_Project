Simple Cupcakes

Ingredients

For The simple cupcakes:

  • 1/2 cup heavy whipping cream (cold)
  • 2 tbsp powdered sugar
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extracts of oil

Classic Vanilla Buttercream Frosting:

  • Prep time: 5 mins
  • Yield: Enough to frost 12 cupcakes generously

Instructions

  1. Whip: Beat the cold heavy cream, powdered sugar, and vanilla extract in a chilled bowl until stiff peaks form.
  2. Core the Cupcakes: Wait until cupcakes are completely cool. Use a small paring knife, apple corer, or large piping tip to cut a small hole(3/4 inch wide) into the center of each cupcake, stopping halfway down. Save the removed cake pieces.
  3. Fill: Pipe or spoon the filling into each hole until level with the top.
  4. Seal & Frost: Trim the bottom off the removed cake pieces and press the top cap back on over the filling, then frost as usual.
